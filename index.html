🐱 LearnUs Planner

A personal study planner for Yonsei University students, made by Alina Tskhay as a class project (IID2103).

It runs on the student's own computer and shows LearnUs deadlines, exams and announcements in a desktop widget, reminds about the Songdo ↔ Sinchon shuttle bus, and can put reminders into the student's own Google Calendar.

Privacy policy

Not an official Yonsei University service.
