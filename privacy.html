Privacy policy — LearnUs Planner

Last updated: October 19, 2026

LearnUs Planner is a desktop program that each student installs and runs on their own computer. There is no server: the author never receives, sees or stores any of your data.

Google data the planner uses

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): the planner creates, updates and removes only the events it made itself (deadlines, exams, classes, shuttle bus reminders) in your primary calendar, so your phone shows notifications. It does not read, change or delete your other events.
  • Gmail, read only (gmail.readonly): the planner reads only messages from senders you choose (by default addresses ending in @yonsei.ac.kr) or with words like "Yonsei" or "UIC" in the subject, and copies their subject and text into the planner on your computer. It never sends, deletes, labels or marks messages as read.

Where data is kept

Everything stays on your computer: the sign-in token (in your user folder), your planner data, and, only if you choose to save it, your Yonsei portal password, which is kept in Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party. Google data is used only to show it to you in the planner and on your phone.

Limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred and not used to train AI models.

Removing access

Press "disconnect" in the planner, or remove LearnUs Planner at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the planner deletes its files.
